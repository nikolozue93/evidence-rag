# AI Usage Disclosure

I used ChatGPT extensively as an implementation and debugging assistant for this take-home task. This included discussing the RAG architecture, generating initial versions of ingestion/retrieval/document-management modules, writing or modifying tests, diagnosing Qdrant and Groq errors, and helping draft the technical documentation. I reviewed and integrated the generated code rather than presenting the project as entirely handwritten.

I independently ran the system in my Fedora development environment, prepared and uploaded the 50,000-record HotpotQA corpus, and checked that multi-document retrieval returned both passages for a known comparison question. I ran the automated tests, investigated the Qdrant payload-index failure during document replacement/deletion, applied the fix, and confirmed that uploading, searching, replacing and deleting documents worked against Qdrant Cloud. I also ran the 40-question retrieval evaluation, 40-question answer evaluation, chunking comparison and controlled insufficient/contradictory-evidence tests. I tested the publicly deployed Render interface with HotpotQA and uploaded documents.

AI assistance was also used to identify and fix citation formatting differences in Groq responses. I ran the offline audit on the original saved answers to verify its reported counts rather than claiming that the original evaluator measured citation validity correctly. The evaluation report distinguishes measured results from proxies and identifies limitations that remain unresolved. The LLM is still used by the **application itself** to generate answers; that is separate from ChatGPT's assistance during development.

With more time, I would first manually review a sample of generated answers to check whether their cited passages actually support the claims. I would also compare retrieval results with different chunking strategies and retrieval limits, and test how the application behaves after restarts or interrupted document updates.
